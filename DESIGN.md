# Oversteer

A browser driving playground, for keyboard and touch, opening directly into a sunlit port district.

The visual signature is a scale-model industrial city with a detailed sage sports sedan, framed by cream instrument panels and a vermilion motorsport wordmark. The environment is the page, rather than a marketing image above the game.

Palette: instrument ivory #f4f2e9, ink #252b29, muted sage #607b70, signal orange #ed6338, sky #b9c9cf, concrete #c4c0b2. Display: condensed Impact/Arial Narrow for motorsport identity. Body: system sans. Instruments: monospace with tabular numerals.

Composition: quiet top navigation; location at top left; camera selector at top right; mini map at lower left; speedometer at lower right; keyboard legend anchored between them. Settings, help, and vehicle customization open accessible native dialogs and pause simulation.

The skill's generic spatial-glass recommendation is adapted to opaque readable instrument surfaces. Color and typography come from automotive instruments and port materials, rather than generic dashboard gradients. Reduced motion disables camera shake and animated UI; touch devices receive driving buttons.
